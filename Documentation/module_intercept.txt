Embedded Wi-Fi regdb overlay
============================

This tree can embed a replacement regdb.bin. The overlay is active whenever
CONFIG_REGDB_FIRMWARE_OVERLAY=y and matches full firmware requests by basename.

The embedded regdb.bin is an imported binary artifact. Its regulatory rules
and provenance cannot be established from source in this tree. Verify its
device-specific Wi-Fi behavior and compliance with applicable local
regulations before shipping a kernel that enables the option.
