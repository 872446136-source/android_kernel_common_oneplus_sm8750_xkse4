Embedded Wi-Fi regdb overlay
============================

This tree can embed a replacement regdb.bin. The overlay is opt-in and is
disabled when its command-line property is missing or has any value other
than "1".

Enable the firmware overlay independently with:

  regdb_overlay.enable=1

The embedded regdb.bin is an imported binary artifact. Its regulatory rules
and provenance cannot be established from source in this tree. Keep the
overlay disabled until its device-specific Wi-Fi behavior and compliance with
the applicable local regulations have been verified.
